## What this file is

It is Thaioil's internal **Work Instruction (WI)** for making their IR (Integrated Report). It shows the whole process, who does each step, and how long each step takes. On the right side there is a special column called **"Note to Oneput"** — that column is Thaioil telling you what they want you to do.

Two stars on the process map show exactly where you come in:

- ⭐ **Yellow star** — the content writing block in Step 4 (IR Content Template Finalization)
- ⭐ **Pink star** — the "Check IR Graphics Layout (TH/EN)" step in Step 5 (IR Design Layout & Production)

So your job is not the whole process. It is **two parts: the writing part and the layout-checking part.**

---

## Part 1 — The content system (Step 4, 40 working days)

They want a platform, not a service. What they ask for:

**Before anything**
- Sign an NDA. Their data is confidential.

**AI writing**
- AI writes the **first draft** using old IR books and material CSSM already published (SD E-news, Akanee).
- AI **updates the content template** to follow IFRS standard.
- After the Content Owner writes their part, AI **cleans it up** — correct, suitable, and nicely written to GRI and IFRS, and good enough to answer sustainability ratings like DJSI, S&P Global, FTSE.
- AI **translates Thai to English**, and it must use a sustainability word list, not normal translation.

**The template becomes an online form**
- Everyone can open it at the same time.
- The form **reminds owners** to come in and fill it.
- **Save and Submit** buttons, with a status that changes colour (red → orange when the owner submits).
- Every Submit **sends an email to CSSM**.
- Every edit has **track changes**, so they can see what was changed.
- It must be **flexible** — an owner can ask to go back and edit again, but must tell CSSM first.

---

## Part 2 — The graphics and report (Step 5, 40 working days)

- Take the numbers inside the text and **turn them into graphs or pictures**.
- Take the Word content and turn it into **graphics + a PDF report**, following the design of Thaioil's past IR books.
- Hard rule: **do not change or cut any content.** Not a single part.

---

## Part 3 — The quality checks your system must pass

Pages 3–5 are basically their QC list. This is how they will judge your work.

**Template must match** the agreed content structure: IR content structure, content flow, disclosure gaps (DJSI/DJBIC gap, SET ESG rating gap), and frameworks (GRI, TCFD, IFRS). Plus a **tracking Excel** so nothing falls behind.

**First draft review:** spelling and grammar, same numbers used across all chapters, numbers are accurate and up to date, content is still relevant (nothing outdated), cross-references between sections/tables/figures/footnotes are correct, DJBIC disclosure gaps are filled, and number tables are turned into graphics or pie charts where suitable.

**Layout review (for every chapter):** content is complete against the AW template, spelling and grammar, no missing letters or lines dropped into the wrong paragraph, performance figures match what the owner gave, and the pictures used are the ones that were sent.

---

## The short version

Thaioil wants Oneput to be **the AI writing engine + the online form workflow + the design production** for their IR report. Roughly: *AI drafts → owners fill an online form with reminders, status and track changes → AI polishes to standards and translates → you turn it into graphics and a PDF that looks like their past reports, without changing a word.*

Two things worth noticing before you scope it: 40 working days per step is tight for a first build, and the pink-star layout job is closer to design production work than software — you may end up needing a vendor relationship or a template system for it, since it repeats every year. Also the doc says it is dated 2022 for illustration, so ask for the current year's content structure ("Item 1" in their appendix) before you promise the template mapping.
